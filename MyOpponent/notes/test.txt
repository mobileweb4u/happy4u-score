## How to test it

1. Open index.html in your browser from happy4u-score-main10.

2. Open the main menu:
   - Click the info icon `ⓘ`.

3. Open `MATCH SETUP`.

4. Enable Bar 8 Champions League:
   - Check `Bar 8 Champions League Active`.
   - Select `JAMIE WHITE [-2] vs Freddie Russell [3]` from the match dropdown.

5. Click `SAVE & START`.

6. Confirm the initial scores:
   - `JAMIE` should start at `-2`
   - `FREDDIE` should start at `3`

7. Play frames normally until the match finishes.

8. Open the report:
   - Click `📊 MATCH SUMMARY`
   - Confirm the modal shows `TYPE: STANDARD RACE TO 11`

9. Also verify the saved report:
   - Click `💾 SAVE` in the summary modal
   - Open the downloaded `.txt`
   - Confirm it also contains `TYPE: STANDARD RACE TO 11`

10. Optional: if you have an archive/export path using generateReport.js, run the same match and verify that tool’s report output also shows `STANDARD RACE TO 11`.

> If you want, I can also give you a quick browser console command to confirm `gameState.raceTo === 11` and the generated report string directly.
