How to test your app
Open the app
Open index.html in your browser or in Acode on Android.
This is the main scoreboard entry point.
Test a league match flow
Select a league match from the dropdown on index.html.
Play the match until one player reaches the race-to target.
Confirm the match ends, the winner modal appears, and the report/save flow is triggered.
Verify completed match persistence
Open ChampionsLeague.html
Confirm the match row changes from ● PENDING to COMPLETED ✅
Confirm the Score column shows the final score
Click the completed row or Review Match button and verify the review modal shows the saved match data
Verify archive browsing
Open archive.html
Confirm completed matches appear in the list
Click VIEW for a completed match
Verify match statistics and score details appear correctly
Use browser dev tools if needed
Open Console to see errors
Check localStorage for key champions_season_history
Confirm completed match entries exist with score, gameState, and timestamp
Repeat for edge cases
Try a draw match if your game supports it
Try a second completed match
Refresh pages to confirm persistence still works
Best test route: index.html → finish a league match → ChampionsLeague.html → check completed score → archive.html → view match stats.
