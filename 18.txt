Yes, exactly! Your understanding is 100% spot on.

Here is the step-by-step flow of what happens before, during, and after a match:

1. Starting the Match (Unchanged)
It starts exactly like before with your MATCH SETUP screen (as shown in your image).

You select player names, set the race length, choose options like Practice Night, Golden Break, etc., and tap SAVE & START.

The live scoring interface opens up and you tap WIN FRAME for either player as frames are played.

2. Playing the Match (100% Offline at the Bar 8 Pool all)
You score the frames live on your Samsung tablet.

You don't need internet, Wi-Fi, or Hotspot active while playing.

3. When Match Criteria Are Met (Winning Score or Draw)
The instant a player hits the winning frame (e.g. 5 wins in a Race to 5):

Background Action: The app silently generates the full terminal match report and archives it into the tablet's local storage for your GAME-MATCH-DAY sheet.

Screen Display: The screen moves directly to the CONGRATULATIONS WINNER modal overlay (without opening or blocking your view with the raw text report popup).

4. Saving & Viewing in your GAME-MATCH-DAY Sheet
Whenever you're ready (either via your iPhone Personal Hotspot at the venue or back home on Wi-Fi):

Open the app menu and tap GAME-MATCH-DAY to export your saved match day data.

Copy/paste or import the data into your GAME-MATCH-DAY Google Sheet.

You or anyone with your shared sheet link can open and view it in real-time on an iPhone, tablet, or PC!
